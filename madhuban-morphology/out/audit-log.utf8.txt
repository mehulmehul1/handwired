=== 3. allometry across the cast ===
  log(limb area) on log(mass):   slope 0.749   r2 0.891   n=13
  log(limb load) on log(mass):   slope 0.251   r2 0.477
  constant bone stress predicts area ~ mass^0.67 and load ~ mass^0.33.
  the cast measures area ~ mass^0.75 at r2 0.89.
  That lands close to the law, and the honest reading is narrow: it means the cast is *consistent with*
  limbs sized by load. It does not mean they were. A body drawn with a single elephant-sized lever and
  then uniformly shrunk produces the same exponent, and so does an artist eyeballing "big animal, stout
  legs". One fit cannot separate those, and the elephant alone may be carrying it ΓÇö drop it and re-fit
  before anyone calls this a law.
  dropped the elephant (which was authored, not inherited): area ~ mass^0.65 at r2 0.76 over 12
  so the inherited bodies alone carry an exponent of 0.65, and the elephant pulls the fit 0.10 up.

=== 4. is it 24 animals or one parameterised animal? ===
  CoM height / body height   mean   0.600   spread   8.6%   range 0.478 .. 0.684
  belly line / body height   mean   0.423   spread  12.0%   range 0.326 .. 0.500
  limb section / h^2         mean   0.040   spread  70.4%   range 0.008 .. 0.118
  footprint / h^2            mean   0.029   spread 107.9%   range 0.004 .. 0.120

  CoM height sits at 60% of body height across 13 species, spread 9%.
  limb section varies 70% around its mean ΓÇö that is real differentiation, not a template.
  So the cast is a low-dimensional *skeleton family* (the PCA found 4 components at 96.7% of a
  115-number vector) with independently authored sculpts on top. The skeleton is generic; the
  surface is not. Both statements are true and they are about different layers.

=== 5. the birds ΓÇö is a wing in the data, or an arm folded against the body? ===

eagle          0.846     0.531          0.381       0.624arm folded along the body
  Two different things are being measured there, and the difference is the finding.
  The sculpt extent is the body including a folded arm; the joint reach is how far the wing arm
  actually gets. Neither is a wingspan, because the eagle sculpt.js says it plainly: "contour
  feathers are part of the body shape; flight feathers are cards, see core/build/featherCards.js".
  So the prim list is a body, not a wing. Span and wing area live in a separate card layer plus a
  posed rig, and no number in this audit can stand in for them. Phase 2 starts there.
